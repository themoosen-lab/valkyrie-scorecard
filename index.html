The Saga

The pair's climb through the Valkyrie tiers. Tap a tier to mark it reached, or tap again to clear it. Passing an exam card and pressing Add pass to the Saga marks it for you.

How the grade works
  • Rate comms, loadouts, resources and stratagems S to E. The mission grade is the lowest of those ratings and the hard limits below. The examiner can overrule it.
  • S needs zero deaths, a full clear, a full extraction and no Liberty's Grace. Not a full clear caps it at A. Not a full extraction caps it at B.
  • Deaths within the limit cap it at A. Needing Liberty's Grace to get back under the limit, or using it twice, caps it at B. Over the limit is C, 8+ deaths D, 10+ deaths E.
  • Liberty's Grace: every 3 caches (Super Credits, Medals, Requisition) cancels 1 death. It can't cancel a death to the feat's own target.
  • Baldr's Chance caps the mission at B, and any death after it fails the pair on the spot. A pair wiped together without it is disqualified.
  • Splitting up without a reason caps it at C. B or better passes. Every mission has to pass, and every listed feat has to be done.
  • Examiner and Assistant deaths never count.
